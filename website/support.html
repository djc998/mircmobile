Support

Help with IRCage, and how to report abuse.

Email support@ircage.com. We read every message and answer abuse reports within 24 hours.

Report someone

In the app, long-press a message (or right-click it on a Mac) and choose Report message, or use Report photo on a photo. The report comes to us and the sender is ignored, so you stop seeing them. To report a person, open their profile and choose Report: it shows the room's operators and network staff, who can act on them directly.

Getting started

  • Pick a nickname and a network. Libera.Chat is a good first network for tech and open source; Undernet and DALnet are more social.
  • Find a room in Discover: rooms are sorted by size, and you can preview one before joining.
  • Star rooms you like so the app rejoins them next time.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Pick a nickname and connect. If you want to keep your nickname, register it with the network from your profile in the app.

Why don't I see a room's old messages?

IRC normally only shows what's said while you're there. Networks and bouncers that keep history (such as Ergo and soju) send what you missed; on others, the app keeps your own history on your device.

Can I use my own server or bouncer?

Yes: on the welcome screen, tap Add your own network and enter its address, port and password. soju bouncers open each of their networks on their own.

Why did a direct (DCC) transfer fail?

Direct transfers connect the two devices to each other, which usually only works on the same Wi-Fi. Send through the server instead: it's encrypted and works on any network.

How do I delete my data?

Saved history: Settings → Chat history → Delete saved history. Photos and files you sent are deleted from our server automatically within hours. Uninstalling the app removes everything it kept on your device.
