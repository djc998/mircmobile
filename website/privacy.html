Privacy policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

In short: IRCage has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your chats go between your device and the IRC networks you choose. Photos and files you send are encrypted on your device before they reach our server, and deleted within hours.

Who we are

IRCage is an app for IRC (Internet Relay Chat) on iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, Windows and Linux. "We" means the people who make IRCage. You can reach us at support@ircage.com.

IRC networks

IRC is run by independent networks such as Libera.Chat and OFTC, not by us. When you connect, the app talks directly to the network you picked. That network sees your IP address, nickname, username and real name (if you set one), and everything you send there. Messages in rooms are public to everyone in the room, and networks have their own privacy policies and rules. We can't see, store or delete what you write on IRC.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, nickname, favorite rooms and the networks you added.
  • Saved chat history, if you leave it on. You can turn it off or delete it in Settings.
  • Passwords for account login and servers, kept in your device's secure keychain.

None of this is sent to us.

Photos and files you send

When you send a photo or file in a private chat, the app encrypts it on your device (AES-256) before uploading it to our server. The key, and a file's name, are only in the link you send, in a part that is never sent to the server. We store an unreadable blob that we can't open.

  • Photos are deleted the first time they're opened, or after 1 or 4 hours, whichever comes first. Before upload, the app removes location and other details from photos.
  • Files are deleted after 1 or 4 hours.

Direct file transfers (DCC) go straight between the two devices and never reach our server; they show each side the other's IP address, and the app tells you so before you accept.

Calls

Voice and video calls are set up through our server and then go directly between the two devices, or through a relay if you turn on "Hide my IP address on calls". Calls are encrypted and are never recorded.

Reports

If you report a message, photo, file or person, we receive what's needed to review it: the reason and any details you add, the sender's nickname, the network and room, and the message text or the link (without its key). We keep reports as long as needed to deal with them and to prevent abuse.

Our server and hosting

Our photo, file and call server runs on Cloudflare. To run and protect the service (for example, to limit how often someone can upload), Cloudflare processes your IP address when the app talks to our server. We don't keep logs of who sends what, and we don't combine any of this with other data.

Links in messages

When someone sends a link to an image or video, IRCage shows a button to load it; it loads only when you tap it, unless you turn on Appearance → Show images from links automatically. Loading it, or opening any link, connects you to the website it's on, which sees your IP address like any website you visit. IRCage doesn't send that website anything else, and we never see which links you open.

Update checks (computer downloads)

The Mac, Windows and Linux apps downloaded from this website check for new versions when they start and once a day, by asking GitHub (where the downloads are published) for the latest release. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any website; nothing else is sent. You can turn this off in Settings → About → Check for updates. Apps from the App Store and Google Play don't do this.

Permissions

  • Camera and microphone: for photos you take and for calls.
  • Photos: to choose a photo to send.
  • Local network: for direct (DCC) file transfers on the same Wi-Fi.

The app uses them only when you use those features.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or tracking, and no advertising.
  • We don't sell or share your information.

Children

IRC is a public, open chat system and IRCage isn't meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. Big changes will also be shown in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@ircage.com.
