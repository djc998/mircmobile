Everything IRC can do, finally easy

The rooms, the people and the culture of IRC, in an app that feels like it belongs on your phone.

Discover rooms

Browse every room on a network by size and topic, preview it, and join with one tap. Star favorites to rejoin them next time.

A chat that reads well

Grouped messages, replies, mentions, typing indicators and classic mIRC colours, always kept readable in light and dark mode.

Every network

Libera.Chat, OFTC, EFnet, Undernet, DALnet and Rizon are one tap away. Add your own server, and stay on several at once.

Private photos and files

Send photos and files in private chats, encrypted on your device. Links stop working after a few hours, and photos can be seen once.

Voice and video calls

Call someone straight from a private chat. Calls can go through a relay, so the other person never sees your IP address.

Never miss a thing

Works with bouncers like soju: get back what you missed, scroll into older messages, and keep your place in sync across devices.

Safe by default

  • Offensive words hidden, adult rooms left out of Discover
  • Ignore anyone from their profile
  • Report messages, photos and people; we review reports within 24 hours

Yours, not ours

No IRCage account, no ads and no tracking. Your chats travel between you and the IRC networks; photos and files are encrypted before they leave your device. Read the privacy policy.
