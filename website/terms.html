What IRCage is

IRCage connects you to IRC, a public chat system run by independent networks. We don't run those networks or their rooms, and we can't see or remove what people write there. Each network has its own rules and staff, and each room has its own operators.

No tolerance for abuse

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive people. Don't post or send anything illegal, hateful, harassing, sexual involving minors, threatening, or meant to deceive or spam. Accounts and rooms that do may be reported to their network, and photos and files that break these rules are deleted from our server.

Keeping yourself safe

Offensive words are hidden and adult rooms are left out of Discover; you can change this in Settings → Safety. Ignore anyone from their profile card to hide their messages, photos and invitations. Report a message, a photo or a person from its menu.

Reports

Reports of messages, photos and files come to us. We review them within 24 hours, delete photos and files that break these rules, and pass abuse on to the network's staff. Room operators and network staff can act on people directly; Report on a profile shows who to contact.

Photos, files and calls

Photos and files are encrypted on your device and deleted from our server when they expire; photos are also deleted when first viewed, and are stripped of location and other details. Direct (DCC) transfers go between the two devices and show each side the other's IP address. Calls are directly between devices, or through a relay if you hide your IP address.

Your privacy

See the privacy policy for what the app keeps on your device and what reaches our server.
